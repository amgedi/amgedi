## 🐈 Currently building: OpenFHS

<div align="center">

<a href="https://github.com/amgedi/OpenFHS">
  <img src="https://github-readme-stats.vercel.app/api/pin/?username=amgedi&repo=OpenFHS&theme=tokyonight&hide_border=true" />
</a>

</div>

**OpenFHS began with Feline Hyperesthesia Syndrome and is growing toward an Open Feline Health Standard.**

I'm building it around structured observation, uncertainty, provenance, missingness, privacy, and open data portability.

> Experimental open-source alpha. Not diagnostic AI, veterinary advice, a veterinary service, clinical validation, or a research study.
